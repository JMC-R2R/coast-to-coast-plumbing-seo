Answer-first snippet: Your main water shut-off is almost always at the water meter, usually in a small pit near the front boundary of the property, sometimes near the front tap. Turn the tap or lever a quarter to half turn until it stops — most modern meters use a lever valve that only needs a quarter turn. Some homes also have an internal stopcock, often under the kitchen sink or near the hot water system, which can isolate the house without affecting the meter.
